About Nuskha

Nuskha is pharmacy management software built in Pakistan for the way Pakistani pharmacies actually work: medicines sold by the strip and the box, credit for regular customers, batches and expiry dates on every shelf, load-shedding and patchy internet, and staff who change shifts.

We built Nuskha around three promises:

  • Your counter never stops. Nuskha on your PC keeps selling without internet.
  • Your records are yours. They stay on your PC (or your own cloud account), encrypted every night, and money records can never be edited or deleted.
  • It speaks your language. Every screen works in English and Urdu.
