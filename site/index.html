Everything Your Pharmacy Needs

From the first bill of the morning to the owner's report at night.

Real-time inventory tracking

Stock levels for every medicine and batch, expiry alerts, reorder levels and a dead-stock report.

Barcode and batch management

Scan to bill. Every batch with its expiry; the earliest expiry sells first.

Smart billing and invoicing

POS billing by strip, box or tablet; cash, Easypaisa, JazzCash, card or bank; prescription details on Rx medicines.

Profit and sales reports

Daily revenue, margins, profit by medicine and top sellers. Export to Excel.

Multi-user roles

Salesman, pharmacist, manager and owner, each with their own PIN and access. Only the owner sees cost and profit.

Local or Cloud, both secure

Local keeps encrypted nightly backups on your PC; Cloud lets owners check in from anywhere. Signed updates and a full audit log.

Customer management

Customer profiles with phone and account number, linked to their bills and their udhaar account.

Manufacturer records

Every medicine linked to its company and generic, so you can find alternatives and trace stock.

Supplier management

Supplier records, purchase orders, deliveries by batch, supplier balances and returns.

Udhaar ledger

Credit for customers and suppliers: limits, payments due, statements and reminders.

Prescriptions and controlled drugs

Doctor and patient details on prescription medicines; controlled drugs need the pharmacist's PIN and are kept in a register.

Inventory and purchases

Purchase records, incoming stock, opening stock and stock counts. Import your whole list from Excel.

Sales and reports

Every sale linked to stock and customer: sales, returns, shifts, cash drawers and expenses.

Settings and hotkeys

User roles, receipt and rounding rules, and keyboard shortcuts (F2 search, F12 pay) for faster sales.
