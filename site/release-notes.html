Release Notes

Updates reach Nuskha on your PC by themselves at night, after a backup.

Version 1 · in development (beta)

Nuskha is still being built with pharmacies. Everything below is in version 1; new things are added to it as they are ready.

  • Counter billing by strip, box or tablet; stock with batches and expiry, earliest expiry sold first; purchases and suppliers; reports; roles and PINs; English and Urdu.
  • Udhaar ledger with limits, statements and one-click WhatsApp reminders: the customer's name, what they owe and for how many days, signed with your pharmacy's WhatsApp number. "Send next" goes through everyone who is due.
  • On WhatsApp: payment receipts with the balance left, bills from the counter, refill reminders for regular medicines, low-stock lists to the supplier who last delivered, and the owner's daily summary. A "WhatsApp today" card on the dashboard. Messages in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, in your own words if you like.
  • Import your medicines with stock (quantity, batch, expiry and cost) from Excel; fast search in large pharmacies (10,000+ medicines).
  • Nuskha Local keeps selling without internet; backups every night are encrypted; updates install by themselves at night after a backup.
  • Nuskha Cloud: open your pharmacy from anywhere, with a 6-digit authenticator code for the owner.
  • Security: strict browser protections on every screen; automatic tests of setup, sales, licences, backups and WhatsApp before every release.
