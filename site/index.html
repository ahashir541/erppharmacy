Questions

Frequently asked

Does MedFlow work without internet?

Yes. MedFlow Local sells, prints bills and keeps stock with no internet. It checks its licence with us when the internet is available; it can stay offline for up to 15 days at a time.

Where is my data kept?

With MedFlow Local, on your own PC, backed up every night in encrypted form. We only see your licence, invoices and simple counts (for example, how many bills), never your sales, customers or stock. With MedFlow Cloud, your records are in our secure cloud database, separate from every other pharmacy.

What happens if I stop paying?

After a grace period MedFlow becomes read-only: you can still see and export everything, but new sales stop. Nothing is deleted.

Can I bring my existing medicine list?

Yes. Import your medicines and stock from Excel or CSV. Columns from most distributor and pharmacy files are recognised automatically.

What computer do I need?

Any PC with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A barcode scanner and a thermal receipt printer (58 or 80 mm) are optional and work like a keyboard and printer.

Can I use more than one counter?

Yes. Other PCs in the shop open MedFlow over the shop's Wi-Fi and are linked once with the owner's PIN.

Is it in Urdu?

Yes, the whole screen switches between English and Urdu.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Updates install by themselves at night (between 1 and 5 am), after a backup. If an update ever fails, MedFlow returns to the previous version by itself.
