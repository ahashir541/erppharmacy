**Between:** [Your company name] ("MedFlow") and the pharmacy named in the licence ("the Pharmacy").

## 1. The service
MedFlow licenses the Pharmacy to use MedFlow Local on its own computers, with MedFlow HQ for
licensing, billing, updates and support. The Pharmacy's records stay on its own computers.

## 2. Licence
- One licence key per pharmacy; branches, counters and staff as allowed by the chosen plan.
- The licence is not transferable. The Pharmacy must not copy, resell, reverse-engineer or remove
  the licence checks from MedFlow.
- MedFlow keeps all rights in the software. The Pharmacy keeps all rights in its own data.

## 3. Trial, fees and payment
- New pharmacies get a free trial of [N] days.
- After the trial, fees are as shown on the invoice (monthly or yearly), payable within [7] days.
- If an invoice is unpaid: a grace period of [N] days, then **read-only** (the Pharmacy can still
  see and export everything but cannot sell), starting at closing time, never mid-day.
- Prices may change with [30] days' notice through MedFlow announcements.

## 4. The Pharmacy's responsibilities
- Keep its PC, Windows and antivirus up to date, and switch on disk encryption (BitLocker) where available.
- Keep PINs secret and give staff only the access they need.
- Keep its **backup key** safe and keep a backup copy off the PC (USB/Drive).
- Use MedFlow lawfully, including the Drugs Act, DRAP rules and prescription requirements.
- Check bills, prices and stock; MedFlow is a tool and does not replace a pharmacist's judgement.

## 5. Our responsibilities
- Provide updates and fixes, and support as described in the SLA and Support Policy.
- Keep MedFlow HQ secure and keep the Pharmacy's account data private (see the Privacy Policy).

## 6. Data
- The Pharmacy owns its data and can export it at any time.
- When the Pharmacy leaves, its data stays on its PC; on request we delete its account from MedFlow HQ.

## 7. Limits of liability
To the extent the law allows, MedFlow is not liable for indirect losses (lost profit, lost
business). Our total liability in any 12 months is limited to the fees the Pharmacy paid in that
period. Nothing limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

## 8. Suspension and ending
- The Pharmacy can stop at any time; fees already paid are not refunded unless agreed.
- We may suspend a licence for non-payment (see 3) or serious misuse, with notice where possible.

## 9. Law and disputes
Governed by the laws of Pakistan. Disputes go first to good-faith discussion for [30] days, then to
the courts of [Karachi].
