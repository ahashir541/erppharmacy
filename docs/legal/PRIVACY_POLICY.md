## 1. Who this covers

- **Pharmacies** that use MedFlow (our customers), and their owners and staff.
- **Patients and customers of those pharmacies**, whose details a pharmacy records in MedFlow.

For patients' and customers' data, **the pharmacy is responsible** (it decides what to record and
why). MedFlow provides the software. For the pharmacy's own account with us (licence, invoices,
payments), **MedFlow is responsible**.

## 2. Where the data lives

| Data | Where it is kept | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Sales, stock, medicines, prices, suppliers | On the pharmacy's own PC (MedFlow Local) | The pharmacy, by role and permission |
| Customers, phone numbers, udhaar, prescriptions | On the pharmacy's own PC | The pharmacy, by role and permission |
| Backups | On the pharmacy's PC, and any USB/Drive folder the pharmacy chooses; **encrypted** (AES-256) | The pharmacy (and anyone with its backup key) |
| Pharmacy name, owner name, phone, city, plan, invoices, payments | MedFlow HQ (run by us) | MedFlow staff |
| Daily check-in: app version, PC name, counts (number of bills, products, staff) | MedFlow HQ | MedFlow staff |
| The pharmacy's backup key | MedFlow HQ (kept so we can help restore a backup on a new PC) | MedFlow staff, only to help that pharmacy |

**MedFlow HQ never receives** sale amounts, patient or customer names, phone numbers, prescriptions
or stock details.

## 3. Why we use it

- To run the software for the pharmacy (contract).
- To issue licences, send invoices and record payments (contract).
- To show the pharmacy update notices and announcements (contract).
- To keep the service secure and to fix problems (legitimate interest).
- To keep records required by law (legal obligation).

We do **not** sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with other pharmacies.

## 4. Who we share it with

Only with: payment channels the pharmacy chooses (bank, JazzCash, Easypaisa) when it pays us;
our hosting provider [name, if HQ moves to a cloud server]; Google, only to sign our own staff
into MedFlow HQ; and authorities when the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Pharmacy records on their PC: as long as the pharmacy keeps them. Medicine sale and purchase
  records should be kept for the period the Drugs rules require ([confirm: usually several years]).
- Our account records (invoices, payments): [7] years, for tax.
- When a pharmacy leaves, we delete its account from MedFlow HQ on request. Its own PC keeps its data.

## 6. Security

PIN and passwords are stored only as strong hashes; staff see only what their role allows; money
records cannot be edited or deleted, only reversed; every action is logged; backups are encrypted;
licences and updates are digitally signed; MedFlow HQ uses HTTPS and Google sign-in for its owner.
See the security policy at `/security-policy` on MedFlow HQ.

## 7. Your rights

Pharmacy owners can ask us for a copy of their account data, a correction, or deletion of their
account. Patients and customers should ask **their pharmacy**, which can view, correct and export
their records in MedFlow. Contact: [email / phone].

## 8. Changes

We will tell pharmacies about important changes in MedFlow (announcements) at least [30] days before they apply.
