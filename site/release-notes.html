Release Notes

Updates reach Nuskha on your PC by themselves at night, after a backup.

Version 0.2 · November 2026

  • New name: Nuskha, with a new icon.
  • Import your medicines with stock (quantity, batch, expiry and cost) from Excel.
  • Counter search is up to 3× faster in large pharmacies (10,000+ medicines).
  • Backups are now encrypted; your backup key is shown in Settings.
  • PIN is typed on the keyboard; clearer licence messages; owners can set their own counter PIN.
  • Nuskha Cloud: sign in from anywhere with a 6-digit authenticator code.
  • Security: stricter browser protections on every screen; automatic tests of setup, sales, licences and backups before every release.

Version 0.1

  • Counter billing, stock with batches and expiry, purchases, udhaar ledger, reports, roles and PINs, English and Urdu.
