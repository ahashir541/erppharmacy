About Nuskha

Pharmacy software made in Pakistan, for Pakistan

Nuskha is built for the way Pakistani pharmacies actually work: medicines sold by the strip and the box, udhaar for regular customers, batches and expiry dates on every shelf, load-shedding and patchy internet, and staff who change shifts.

Our vision

Every pharmacy in Pakistan running on records it can trust

From a single counter in a small town to a chain across cities, we want every pharmacy owner to know their stock, their money and their customers at a glance, and never lose a sale, a rupee or a patient's trust to guesswork.

Our mission

Simple, reliable software priced for Pakistani pharmacies

We make pharmacy software that any counter can learn in a day, keeps selling without internet, keeps the owner in control of stock, money and data, and comes with real help from people who understand pharmacies here.

What we promise

Your counter never stops

Nuskha Local keeps selling without internet or during load-shedding.

Your records are yours

On your own PC or your own cloud account, backed up and encrypted. Money records can never be edited or deleted.

It speaks your language

Every screen works in English and Urdu.

Honest numbers

Profit, udhaar and stock straight from your own sales and purchases.
