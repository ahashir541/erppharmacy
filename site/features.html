POS & billing

Fast billing at the counter

Made for a busy counter: the keyboard does almost everything.

  • Search by name, generic or barcode (F2), sell by strip, box or tablet
  • The batch that expires first is sold first, automatically
  • Cash, card, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank or udhaar; hold a bill, take returns
  • Prescription details for Rx medicines; prints on 58 or 80 mm paper (F12)

Expiry

Stop money expiring on the shelf

See what is about to expire while there is still time to sell or return it.

  • Batches expiring within 7, 30, 60 and 90 days, with quantity and rack
  • Expired and recalled batches can never be sold
  • Write off expired stock with a record of who did it
  • The earliest expiry is always sold first

Give credit to regular customers without losing track of a rupee.

  • Khata accounts with credit limits; over-limit udhaar is blocked

WhatsApp

Reminders in one click

Nuskha writes the message and opens WhatsApp; you press Send.

  • Udhaar reminders with the name, amount and how many days, from your own number
  • "Send next" goes through everyone who is due
  • Payment receipts, bills, refill reminders and low-stock lists for suppliers
  • The owner's daily summary, in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English
