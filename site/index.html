Two ways to use Nuskha

Nuskha Local or Nuskha Cloud — you choose

Same billing, stock, expiry, udhaar and reports in both. The only difference is where your records are kept.

Way 1

Nuskha Local

Installed on your shop PC

  • Every record stays on your own shop PC
  • Keeps selling when the internet is down
  • Other counters join over the shop's Wi-Fi
  • Encrypted backup every night; updates install at night
  • Needs internet once in 15 days for the licence

Best for: a pharmacy that wants its data in the shop and works from one place.

Download Nuskha Local
Way 2

Nuskha Cloud

Opens in your browser

  • Nothing to install; works on a PC, tablet or phone
  • Check sales and stock from home or anywhere
  • Records kept safe on cloud servers, backed up daily
  • Owner signs in with password + 6-digit app code
  • Needs a working internet connection to sell

Best for: an owner who wants to see the pharmacy from anywhere, or runs more than one branch.
