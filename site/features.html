Dashboard

Your whole pharmacy on one screen

Open Nuskha and see how the day is going before you even reach the counter.

  • Sales and profit for today, this week and this month
  • Cash in the drawers, udhaar to collect, what you owe suppliers
  • Stock expiring in the next 90 days and dead stock, at cost
  • "WhatsApp today": reminders, refills and low stock waiting for you
Request a Demo
POS & billing

Fast billing at the counter

Made for a busy counter: the keyboard does almost everything.

  • Search by name, generic or barcode (F2), sell by strip, box or tablet
  • The batch that expires first is sold first, automatically
  • Cash, card, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank or udhaar; hold a bill, take returns
  • Prescription details for Rx medicines; prints on 58 or 80 mm paper (F12)
Request a Demo
Stock & batches

Every medicine, every batch

Know exactly what is on your shelves and where.

  • Each medicine with its generic, company, rack and pack sizes
  • Batches with expiry dates and quantity, live as you sell
  • Reorder levels, stock counts and a full history of stock movements
  • Import your whole list, with stock, from Excel in minutes
Request a Demo
Expiry

Stop money expiring on the shelf

See what is about to expire while there is still time to sell or return it.

  • Batches expiring within 7, 30, 60 and 90 days, with quantity and rack
  • Expired and recalled batches can never be sold
  • Write off expired stock with a record of who did it
  • The earliest expiry is always sold first
Request a Demo
Customers & udhaar

Udhaar under control

Give credit to regular customers without losing track of a rupee.

  • Khata accounts with credit limits; over-limit udhaar is blocked
  • What every customer owes, what is overdue and what is over 90 days
  • Statements, payments and unpaid bills for each customer
  • Regular medicines for refill reminders
Request a Demo
WhatsApp

Reminders in one click

Nuskha writes the message and opens WhatsApp; you press Send.

  • Udhaar reminders with the name, amount and how many days, from your own number
  • "Send next" goes through everyone who is due
  • Payment receipts, bills, refill reminders and low-stock lists for suppliers
  • The owner's daily summary, in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English
Request a Demo
Suppliers & purchases

Buying made simple

From the order to the delivery to the payment, in one place.

  • Purchase orders sent to the supplier on WhatsApp
  • Deliveries received by batch and expiry, bonus included
  • Supplier invoices, returns and what you owe each supplier
  • Payments by cash, bank or cheque
Request a Demo
Reports

The numbers that matter

Every report opens in one click and exports to Excel or PDF.

  • Sales by day, medicine, staff, hour and payment method
  • Profit by medicine (only the owner sees cost and profit)
  • Stock value at cost and retail, and dead stock
  • Purchases by supplier, udhaar collections and staff performance
Request a Demo
Staff & security

Every sale has a name

Each person signs in with their own PIN and sees only what their role allows.

  • Salesman, pharmacist, manager and owner roles
  • Only the owner sees cost, profit and money totals
  • Every sale, return and discount saved with who did it
  • Encrypted nightly backups; money records can never be edited or deleted
