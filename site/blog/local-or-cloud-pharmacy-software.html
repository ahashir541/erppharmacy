Local or cloud: which pharmacy software is right for you?

By Syed Hashir Ali, Founder of Nuskha · 16 Nov 2026

"Cloud" sounds modern, but for a pharmacy the right answer depends on one question first: can your counter afford to stop when the internet does?

Software on your PC (local)

  • Keeps selling with no internet — important with load-shedding and patchy connections.
  • Your records stay inside your shop.
  • You need a reliable main PC and a backup copy off the PC (USB or Google Drive).

Cloud software

  • The owner can check sales and stock from home or on a phone.
  • Several branches in one place; nothing to install.
  • The counter needs internet to sell.

How to choose

A single pharmacy with uncertain internet is usually best served by local software with good backups. A chain, or an owner who manages from a distance, often prefers the cloud. Nuskha offers both, with the same screens, so you can choose what fits. Ask us which suits your pharmacy.
