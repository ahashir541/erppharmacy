Install and set up Nuskha

From download to your first bill in about ten minutes. Everything a pharmacy needs, step by step.

1

Download

On the main PC of the pharmacy (Windows 10 or 11), open nuskhapk.vercel.app → Download and save NuskhaSetup.exe (about 52 MB).

Edge or Chrome may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded": click … → Keep.
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Nuskha is new, so Windows does not know it yet. Click More info → Run anyway. When Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?", click Yes.
2

Install (one minute)

Double-click NuskhaSetup.exe and press Install. Setup puts Nuskha in C:\Nuskha, adds a Nuskha icon to the desktop, makes Nuskha start by itself every time the PC is switched on, and lets the shop's other PCs reach it. Then press Open Nuskha.

Already have Nuskha? Running Setup again updates it and keeps all your records, backups and settings.

3

Set up your pharmacy (three short screens)

Choose Set up my pharmacy. You fill in three short screens; nothing else is needed.

Press Start using Nuskha. You are now the owner: only your PIN opens the back office, cost and profit.

4

Your licence key

Nuskha sends you a licence key for your plan (for example MF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) on WhatsApp or email. Enter it on the last setup screen, or later in Settings → Nuskha licence within 7 days. You will see Licence key verified and your plan.

  • Nuskha keeps working without internet. It needs to reach Nuskha once every 15 days to renew the licence; then it stays licensed.
  • Your plan, trial days and invoices are shown in Settings → Nuskha licence. If a payment is late, Nuskha becomes read-only (you can see everything, but not sell) until it is paid.
5

Staff, PINs and other counters

Team → Add: each salesman, pharmacist or manager gets their own name and PIN. Every sale is saved with who made it. Only the owner sees cost, profit and money totals.

A second or third counter PC (same shop Wi-Fi or network): open the address shown in Settings → Other counters open (for example http://192.168.1.5:5199) in Chrome or Edge, and press Link this PC with the owner's PIN. If the other PC cannot reach it, run NuskhaSetup.exe once more on the main PC (it opens the network for Nuskha).

6

Your medicines and stock

Products & stock → Import: bring your whole medicine list from Excel, with stock, batch, expiry and cost. Or add medicines one by one. After that, every sale and purchase updates the stock by itself.

7

WhatsApp reminders

Open WhatsApp & reminders → Setup, enter the pharmacy's WhatsApp number and choose the language (Urdu, Roman Urdu or English). Then:

  • Udhaar reminders: every customer who owes, how much and for how many days. Send next opens WhatsApp with the message written; you press Send.
  • Payment receipts after udhaar is paid, bills from the counter, refill reminders for regular medicines, low-stock lists for suppliers, and the owner's daily summary.
  • WhatsApp (the app or WhatsApp Web) must be signed in on that PC or phone.
8

Backups: where they are and how to keep them safe

  • Automatic: Nuskha backs up the whole pharmacy every night at 11 pm on the main PC, and keeps the last 30.
  • Where: in C:\Nuskha\backups. The Nuskha Backups icon on the desktop opens that folder. Files look like medflow-2026-11-20_230000.tar.gz.enc.
  • Now: Settings → Back up now makes one at once (do it before moving or repairing the PC).
  • A second copy (important): in Settings → Second copy of every backup, enter a USB drive or a Google Drive / OneDrive folder (for example E:\Nuskha backups or G:\My Drive\Nuskha) and press Save. From then on every backup is also saved there, so a lost, stolen or broken PC does not take your records with it.
  • Encrypted: backups are locked with your backup key (MFBK-…, shown in Settings). Nobody can open them without it. Write the key down and keep it safe; Nuskha keeps a copy too, so we can help you.
9

New PC, or bringing a backup back

  1. On the new PC, install Nuskha with NuskhaSetup.exe (do not set up a new pharmacy).
  2. Copy your newest backup file from the USB / Google Drive into C:\Nuskha\backups.
  3. Right-click C:\Nuskha\Restore-Backup.ps1 → Run with PowerShell, choose the backup, type the backup key, and type YES.
  4. Nuskha starts with all your records: medicines, stock, customers, udhaar, sales and staff. Enter your licence key if asked.

Prefer help? Send us a WhatsApp message and we will do it with you. The data that was on the PC before a restore is kept aside, never deleted.

10

Updates

Nothing to do. New versions install by themselves at night (1–5 am), after a backup. If anything goes wrong, Nuskha goes back to the version that worked. Settings → Nuskha version shows what you have.

11

If something is wrong

  • Nuskha does not open: wait a minute after switching the PC on; then use the desktop icon. Still not? Restart the PC.
  • Electricity or internet went off: nothing is lost. Every sale is saved at once; Nuskha keeps selling offline.
  • Another counter cannot connect: both PCs must be on the same Wi-Fi/network; run NuskhaSetup.exe once more on the main PC.
  • A staff member forgot their PIN: the owner sets a new one in Team. If the owner forgets, contact Nuskha.
  • Anything else: WhatsApp us. Send a photo of the screen and, if asked, the file C:\Nuskha\logs\medflow.log.
