Back office ke pages, file ba file:

Office.tsx     = Sidebar (pages ke naam aur tarteeb), shortcuts.
sales.tsx      = Dashboard, Sales & invoices, Shifts.
parties.tsx    = Customers & udhaar (khata), Suppliers.
whatsapp.tsx   = WhatsApp & reminders.
stock.tsx      = Products & stock, Add product, Opening stock, Expiry, Stock movements.
counts.tsx     = Shelf check (stock count).
purchases.tsx  = Purchases: Receive delivery, orders, returns to supplier.
money.tsx      = Expenses, Accounts & books, Reports.
admin.tsx      = Team, Settings, Approvals & alerts.
roles.tsx      = Role ki permissions.
importer.tsx   = Excel se products import.
localserver.tsx= Settings mein licence, backups, doosre PC jodna.
office.css     = Back office ka design.
ui.tsx         = Chhote hisse (table, modal) jo sab pages use karte hain. Na chhedein.
