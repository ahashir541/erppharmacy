Keeping udhaar under control without losing customers

Udhaar is part of how neighbourhood pharmacies keep loyal families. The trouble starts when it lives in a diary: amounts get missed, nobody knows the total, and asking for money feels awkward.

Give every customer a limit

Decide in advance how much credit each regular customer can have. When the limit is reached, the counter knows to ask for a payment before giving more.

Record payments the moment they come in

A payment written down later is a payment that gets forgotten. Record it at the counter, against the customer's account, with the method (cash, Easypaisa, JazzCash).

Share a clear statement

Customers pay faster when they can see exactly what they bought and when. A short statement removes arguments.

Know your total udhaar every day

The owner should see one number every morning: how much money is out with customers. If it keeps rising, tighten limits.
